Name: CueFlora — AI Subtitles & Translation for YouTube

Short description: YouTube subtitles and translation using your chosen AI services. Requires the CueFlora helper for AI generation.

Detailed description:

CueFlora brings source subtitles and translations together on YouTube. Use available video captions, or connect your own compatible transcription and translation services to generate subtitles after you press Run.

Choose languages and adjust subtitle appearance, position, background, word highlighting and Chinese pinyin. AI stages are configured independently; no provider, endpoint or model is preselected. You can postpone AI setup and use captions supplied by the video.

AI generation needs a separate Windows x64 CueFlora helper. Transcription temporarily downloads the video's audio, prepares it and sends it to the transcription service you choose. Translation sends subtitle text and limited video context to your selected translation service. The first helper installer is unsigned and Windows may warn. Local services must already be running; CueFlora does not install models or provide a hosted AI subscription. Metadata connection checks do not guarantee service compatibility. Provider fees, limits and retention policies are their own.

The extension does not generate AI subtitles until you press Run. Caption availability and recognition/translation accuracy depend on the video and configured services. This is an independent project derived from Substrata, not affiliated with YouTube, Google or AI-service providers.
